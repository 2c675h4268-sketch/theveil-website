# The Veil — AI Crawler & Discovery Policy

_Reference doc. Last reviewed: 2026-07-06._

## Business model → guiding principle

The Veil is a **free weekly newsletter** — the business is **audience growth / lead-gen**, not
subscriptions, ads, or ecommerce. The site is a **discovery funnel**: its job is to be found, read,
and convert visitors into confirmed email subscribers. Therefore **discoverability outweighs content
protection** for almost everything. The one thing worth protecting is the original editorial work
(credibility scores + "so what" analysis) from being absorbed into model *training* with no
attribution or referral.

## The policy

This matches the house standard set on **The Morning Current** (`themorningcurrent.com/robots.txt`) — welcome search + AI answers + agents, block only the pure training scrapers.

| Bot class | Stance | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| **Search** (Googlebot, Bingbot, Applebot, DuckDuckBot) | **Allow all** | Search is the oxygen supply. Never block. |
| **AI answers / assistants / agents** (OAI-SearchBot, ChatGPT-User, PerplexityBot, Perplexity-User, ClaudeBot, Claude-User, Google-Extended, meta-externalagent) | **Allow** | These power cited AI answers (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Gemini/AI Overviews, Meta AI) — they attribute + send referral traffic. Being *the* cited source is exactly the "credibility scorer" positioning. |
| **Pure training / bulk scrapers** (GPTBot, CCBot, Bytespider, Amazonbot, Applebot-Extended) | **Block** | Absorb content for training with no attribution or referral. Blocking costs zero discoverability. |

**Content signals (robots.txt):** `search=yes, ai-input=yes, ai-train=no, use=reference`.

### Page-level rules
- **Maximize discovery** (search + assistants): homepage `/`, evergreen hubs
  `/casefiles` `/watch` `/map` `/new-to-uap` `/methodology` `/standards`, `/archive`, and all
  `/story/<slug>` pages. The evergreen hubs are the crown jewels — people search those queries
  directly and AI assistants favor reference-style pages.
- **Stricter (noindex)**: `/confirmed` (transactional), `mockup.html` + `newsletter-preview.html`
  (internal dev artifacts). No login/account/paywall pages exist.

### Risks to keep in mind
- **Blocking too much:** blocking search = invisible on Google (fatal for a funnel with no other
  acquisition channel); blocking assistants = never cited as "ask an AI" replaces search; and it's
  easy to fat-finger `Applebot` (search) when you meant `Applebot-Extended` (training).
- **Allowing too much:** allowing *training* gives away the editorial judgment that's genuinely ours;
  assistants can zero-click summarize an answer without a visit (accepted trade-off while growing —
  revisit if content is ever gated or monetized by pageview).

## Where each control lives (how to change things)

- **robots.txt** = custom file in this repo (`/robots.txt`), mirroring The Morning Current's. **To
  serve it, Cloudflare's Managed robots.txt must be turned OFF** for the theveil.media zone
  (dashboard → AI Crawl Control / Bots → disable managed robots.txt), otherwise Cloudflare injects
  its own at the edge and shadows the repo file. TMC has managed robots.txt off and serves a custom
  file — do the same here.
- **`sitemap.xml`** = generated by `tools/build-stories.py` (`write_sitemap()`), regenerated every
  weekly run. Lists the 8 hubs + every `/story/<slug>`. Served at `/sitemap.xml`.
- **`noindex` on dev/transactional pages** = `<meta name="robots" content="noindex,nofollow">` in the
  page `<head>` (in-repo). Applied to `mockup.html`, `newsletter-preview.html`; `confirmed.html` is
  `noindex`.
- **Canonicals** = story pages canonicalize to the clean `/story/<slug>` URL (not `.html`, which
  307-redirects). Generated by `build-stories.py`; keep the clean form on any new templates.
- **Sitemap submission** = Cloudflare's managed robots.txt usually can't carry a `Sitemap:` line, so
  submit `https://theveil.media/sitemap.xml` in **Google Search Console** + **Bing Webmaster Tools**.

## Note on hosting
theveil.media is served by a **Cloudflare Worker**, not Cloudflare Pages (the only Pages project in
the account is `workaidaily`, which serves themorningcurrent.com). So Cloudflare **Pages Functions
(`/functions/*`) do NOT route** here, and there is no repo-level `robots.txt` override — crawler
controls are dashboard-managed. Server endpoints for The Veil live on Supabase instead.
